# Research method

## Observation, calculation, interpretation

Poolgob keeps three layers visible. Observations are the supplied snapshot and tagged events. Calculations are reserve ratios, quotes, cohort shares and rule measurements. Interpretation is the short research note prepared from those outputs.

The shipped observation layer is a synthetic fixture. The calculations are implemented code. Grok Bot receives the resulting packet through an explicitly configured file workflow; the repo does not claim that an external Bot has already executed a task.

## Why small token pools

In small community and meme-native markets, a familiar ticker or a repeated story can dominate attention. Poolgob's research question stays mechanical: which reserves and classifications support the claimed depth, at the declared ticket size and time cutoff?

This edition does not collect social attention or estimate narrative popularity. The token names are invented reference labels, not endorsements or a list of watched live assets.

## Controlled stress

The depth study scales reserves proportionally at one observation. It is a controlled mathematical stress, not a forecast. The freshness study changes the requested clock around a known missing-data boundary. The cohort study changes one declared classification and recomputes the denominator.

Each study states what changed and what stayed fixed. Results should not be translated into market performance or real account ownership claims.

## Evidence trail

Every finding has measured values, units and source IDs. A case contains the selected snapshot; the versioned reference dataset resolves earlier observations. A local content digest detects an alteration of the serialized case, while leaving source truth and model validity as separate questions.

## Independent validation

Tests check an analytical quote example, zero-fee invariance, retained-fee product growth, monotonic depth effects, source cutoffs, stale withholding, classification effects and receipt consistency. These checks establish behavior of the implementation within its assumptions, not external market accuracy.
