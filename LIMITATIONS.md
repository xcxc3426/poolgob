# Interpretation limits

## Synthetic observations

The included data is generated for this project. Its pool names, account aliases, relative timestamps and tagged events are fictional. It is not scraped from Solana, a live token scanner or a historical market benchmark.

The holder cohort is a stylized balance distribution, not an end-to-end reconstruction of token transfers. The generator records known event causes; a live feed would need independent program-aware attribution.

## Model coverage

Only `cpmm-demo-v1` is supported. Concentrated liquidity, bonding curves, routing, token transfer fees, token-program extensions, priority fees, account rent and exact integer rounding are outside its equations. A real Solana pool may require materially different math.

There is no wallet, signing path, mint deployment, transaction broadcast or order execution. A model output is not a guaranteed fill.

## Partial denominators

Concentration uses the declared modeled eligible cohort. It is not total supply or independent human ownership. An excluded or unknown classification can materially change the result and should not disappear behind a single score.

## Research states

Quiet, observe and review describe rule outcomes. They are not a trained classifier, safety guarantee, valuation model or trading instruction. A pool can have no triggered rule and still have important unmodeled risks.

## Provider workflow

The Grok Bot component is a user-configured role and routine pack. Local artifacts identify their prepared state. A configured external Bot performs work in its own account; the repo does not simulate a completed provider run or claim a partnership.

## Integrity scope

SHA-256 identifies bytes under the declared encoding. It does not authenticate a publisher, certify an observation, prove model correctness or establish an on-chain record. Reproducible output can still reflect an incomplete model.
