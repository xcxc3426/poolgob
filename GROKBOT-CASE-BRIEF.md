# Single-case research brief

Read the current `case.json` and the Poolgob operator role. Produce one concise `research-note.md`.

## Required checks

- The case's source origin and model must be stated.
- The selected observation must not exceed the requested cutoff.
- The nominal ticket and token-position convention must remain explicit.
- Every finding must cite a source ID present in the packet.
- Stale inputs must keep their quote unavailable.
- Cohort concentration must keep its declared denominator.
- A local digest must not be described as a signature or chain record.

If a required field is absent, return the missing field and its effect on the note. Do not create plausible substitute values.

## Suggested note structure

Opening: the most important measured change and its source boundary.

Facts: reserve, ticket, modeled output or withholding, tagged LP activity and cohort scope.

Interpretation: one short paragraph grounded in those facts.

Gaps: the next evidence needed, without a buy/sell instruction.

Return the completed note and its artifact path in the conversation. The packet's prepared state is not evidence of a completed provider run; the conversation result is the actual handoff outcome.
