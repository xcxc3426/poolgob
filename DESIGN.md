# A goblin with a research desk

## Character

Poolgob has cobalt skin, long angular ears, a crooked nose, one small tusk and a copper hood. Its visual language is a chunky retro pixel sprite with a patient, unimpressed expression. It is deliberately distinct from a rounded robot, animal-brain reconstruction, paper-fold creature or neon fly.

The mascot sits on a plain graphite background. It has no coin, money bag, chart or trophy. The connection to markets comes from the workroom and its evidence rather than a trading prop attached to the character.

## Interface language

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Graphite background | `#12171d` |
| Observation panel | `#1a222b` |
| Copper attention | `#e19a64` |
| SOL and fresh measurements | `#6dc9c5` |
| Cohort and indexed traces | `#829ee0` |
| Secondary emphasis | `#d4bd75` |

Copper indicates a condition requiring attention; it does not mean a sell recommendation. Cyan means a measurement is available or belongs to a named series, not that an asset is safe.

## Complex views with explicit jobs

The watchroom combines a trace, event strip, case summary and cross-pool table. The surface is a large stress matrix. The holder view combines ranks and denominator membership. The ledger prioritizes event attribution. The atlas normalizes comparisons. The feed desk makes stale data visible. The receipt isolates the exact byte commitment.

The same navigation anchors the desk, while each composition is built around its own analytical question. Small labels carry units and source boundaries; large values identify the measurement rather than a vague score.

## Text and exports

The prepared figures use DejaVu Sans, a bold face and DejaVu Sans Mono. Numeric text and diagrams are code-rendered, not generated inside image artwork. Native PNGs have a consistent 1600 × 1040 frame; the social cover uses 1600 × 800.

Browser captions, labeled controls, focus styles and CSV/JSON exports supplement the Canvas. They do not constitute a complete screen-reader alternative to every chart or a claim of accessibility conformance.
